Our mission

Agricultural engineering uses engineering for farm power, farm machinery, irrigation, and soil and water conservation. We help farms produce more with less water, labour and energy.

Our objectives:

  • Teach the principles of tractors and farm implements.
  • Train students to plan water-efficient irrigation.
  • Promote safe and low-cost technology for small farms.

Laboratories

  • Farm Machinery Laboratory: tillage tools, seed drills, sprayers and a demonstration tractor.
  • Irrigation and Soil-Water Laboratory: drip and sprinkler units, flow meters and soil-moisture sensors.
  • Farm Power Corner: engine models and a small solar pump.
